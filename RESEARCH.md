## Patterns to carry forward
Immediate entry, one understandable action per experience, distinct illustrated identity for each tile, low navigation overhead, visible feedback, replayability, and a mix of active games and slower toys.

## Indian context
Use recognizable situations rather than recoloring every original game. Chai orders, gully cricket, bargaining, lunch dabbas, rolling a roti, a train window, rupee-scale spending, and property trading provide varied actions. Spend a Crore and Perfect Roti adapt familiar generic mechanics; the remaining games widen the collection's identity.
